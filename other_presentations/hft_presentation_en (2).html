Task

What we predict and how it is scored

  • Every 500 ms: 172,800 points per day per instrument
  • Target: yH(t) = ln(mid(t+10ms+H) / mid(t+10ms)), H ∈ {500 ms, 2 s, 8 s}; 10 ms models order latency
  • 4 target perpetuals; features from 20 instruments: perpetuals, spot pairs, stablecoin rates
  • Strict causality: at time t only data with ts ≤ t; a violation means disqualification

Metric

  1. target clipped: yclip = min(max(y, q0.1%), q99.9%), percentiles of the training period
  2. Pearson correlation per day for each instrument × horizon pair:
    r = Σ(p − p̄)(y − ȳ) / √( Σ(p − p̄)² · Σ(y − ȳ)² )
  3. mean over days → mean over the 12 pairs

r = 1: forecast and return move together exactly; r = 0: no relation. Scale does not matter, only direction and relative size. Our 0.23 → explains ≈ 5% of the variance (0.23² ≈ 0.05).

Features

76 features, all strictly causal

Own order book and trades

  • best-quote and depth imbalances, microprice, spread and their dynamics
  • trade flow, trade counts, time since the last buy / sell, order flow imbalance; windows from 0.5 s to minutes
  • own returns, volatility and interactions with it

Other markets

  • returns of the other perpetuals, "missed moves" between grid points
  • spot: perpetual–spot gap in a common currency via the stablecoin rate
  • the same coin quoted in USDC: spot for BTC/ETH/SOL, the 1000PEPEUSDC perpetual for PEPE

83 features built, 7 removed by selection (next slide). Each one checked bit-for-bit: truncating the data at time T does not change its values before T.

Ensemble

The blend beats every single model

1

Models trained on 09-15 – 04-30 predict the held-out part of the training period, 05-01 – 07-14.

2

Choose the weighting scheme honestly: fit on even days, check on odd days, and vice versa. Global / per horizon / per pair → per pair wins.

3

Fit the weights on all days 05-01 – 07-14: each prediction divided by its std, weights ≥ 0 summing to 1, grid step 0.05, exact metric.

4

Validation 07-15 – 09-14: the weights are applied as they are, nothing is fitted.

The more liquid the market and the shorter the horizon, the better the forecast.

Week of Aug 19–25

Longer horizons dropped for all instruments at once: at 8 s, BTC/ETH/SOL 0.135 vs 0.211 on other days, PEPE 0.083 vs 0.152. 4 of the 5 worst validation days are in this week. It costs 0.0055 of the ensemble's SCORE (0.2401 without it) — a market regime shift, not a model error.

PEPE drift

The 500 ms correlation declines from May: 0.278 → 0.261 → 0.231 (May–July); on validation 0.223 / 0.211 / 0.236 (Jul / Aug / Sep). That is why the final model also learns from July–September.

Weekends

Weekdays vs weekends: 500 ms 0.286 vs 0.234, 2 s 0.253 vs 0.223, but 8 s 0.184 vs 0.197.
